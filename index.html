01 — Features
Everything You Need
Six modules. One platform. No per-seat pricing, no enterprise lock-in.
C
CRM & Pipeline
Contacts, companies, deals, and stages. Drag deals through your pipeline. Track every interaction. AI auto-enriches profiles from public data.
O
OKRs
Set objectives and key results at company, team, and individual levels. Track progress in real time. Agents suggest adjustments when you fall behind.
P
Projects
Kanban boards, timelines, task assignments, and dependencies. Every project links to OKRs so nothing drifts from the mission.
$
Invoices
Generate, send, and track invoices. Recurring billing, payment reminders, and overdue alerts. Connects to RoadCoin for crypto payments.
S
SOPs
Standard operating procedures with version control. Agents follow SOPs autonomously. Checklists, approval flows, and compliance tracking built in.
I
Ideation Board
Capture ideas, vote on them, and promote the best to projects. Brainstorm with AI agents. Every idea gets a thread for discussion and refinement.
